Estefano at Fanovation Builds

A builder and woodworker in New York City.

The practice is three services: antique repair and wood restoration, custom carpentry, and full renovations. He takes work across New York City.

The restoration on this site is work he did as an apprentice at Vollana Antiques. The shop gave permission to show those pieces. They stay credited to Vollana.

Job videos are on the YouTube channel.

To start a job, send a quote request with the neighborhood, the timing, and photos if you have them. He replies to the phone or email on that form.
